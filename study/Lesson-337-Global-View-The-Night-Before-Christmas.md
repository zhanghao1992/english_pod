# Lesson 337 · Global View - The Night Before Christmas（圣诞前夜）

> **等级**：Intermediate（中级，C 系列默认；主持人口播标明为 advanced 高级·圣诞诗歌特辑）　**编号**：C0337　**场景**：Global View（圣诞前夜，父亲讲述圣诞老人驾雪橇来访的经典叙事诗）
> **学习目标**：听懂并诵读英语世界家喻户晓的圣诞诗《A Visit from St. Nicholas》（圣尼古拉来访），掌握 6 个核心表达：
> `'Twas…` / `not a creature was stirring` / `more rapid than…（比较级前置）` / `in spite of oneself` / `chubby and plump` / `dash away`

---

## 一、对话原文（Dialogue）

**N = 叙事者（家中的父亲；主持人讲解确认他与 mama 已上床就寝）**；全诗为他的讲述，引号内是他转述的 St. Nicholas 吆喝驯鹿的口令与结尾祝福。

- **N**: 'Twas the night before Christmas, when all through the house<br>Not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse;<br>The stockings were hung by the chimney with care,<br>In hopes that St. Nicholas soon would be there;
- **N**: The children were nestled all snug in their beds,<br>And mama in her 'kerchief, and I in my cap,<br>Had just settled down for a long winter's nap,
- **N**: When out on the lawn there arose such a clatter,<br>I sprang from the bed to see what was the matter.<br>Away to the window I flew like a flash,<br>Tore open the shutters and threw up the sash.
- **N**: The moon on the breast of the new-fallen snow<br>Gave the lustre of mid-day to objects below,<br>When, what to my wondering eyes should appear,<br>But a miniature sleigh, and eight tiny reindeer,<br>With a little old driver, so lively and quick,<br>I knew in a moment it must be St. Nick.
- **N**: More rapid than eagles his coursers they came,<br>And he whistled, and shouted, and called them by name;<br>"Now, Dasher! now, Dancer! now, Prancer and Vixen!<br>On, Comet! on Cupid! on, Donder and Blitzen!<br>To the top of the porch! to the top of the wall!<br>Now dash away! dash away! dash away all!"
- **N**: As dry leaves that before the wild hurricane fly,<br>When they meet with an obstacle, mount to the sky,<br>So up to the house-top the coursers they flew,<br>With the sleigh full of toys, and St. Nicholas too.
- **N**: And then, in a twinkling, I heard on the roof<br>The prancing and pawing of each little hoof.<br>As I drew in my head, and was turning around,<br>Down the chimney St. Nicholas came with a bound.
- **N**: He was dressed all in fur, from his head to his foot,<br>And his clothes were all tarnished with ashes and soot;<br>A bundle of toys he had flung on his back,<br>And he looked like a peddler just opening his pack.
- **N**: His eyes – how they twinkled! his dimples how merry!<br>His cheeks were like roses, his nose like a cherry!<br>His droll little mouth was drawn up like a bow,<br>And the beard of his chin was as white as the snow;<br>The stump of a pipe he held tight in his teeth,<br>And the smoke it encircled his head like a wreath;
- **N**: He had a broad face and a little round belly,<br>That shook, when he laughed like a bowlful of jelly.<br>He was chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf,<br>And I laughed when I saw him, in spite of myself;
- **N**: A wink of his eye and a twist of his head,<br>Soon gave me to know I had nothing to dread;<br>He spoke not a word, but went straight to his work,<br>And filled all the stockings; then turned with a jerk,<br>And laying his finger aside of his nose,<br>And giving a nod, up the chimney he rose;
- **N**: He sprang to his sleigh, to his team gave a whistle,<br>And away they all flew like the down of a thistle.<br>But I heard him exclaim, ere he drove out of sight,<br>"Happy Christmas to all, and to all a good-night."

> **PDF 勘误**：① 已修复 pdftotext 断词断行（bythe→by the、onthe→on the、ofthe→of the、tothe→to the、wasthe→was the、whis-tle→whistle）及 "St.　Nicholas" 的异常字距；② PDF 第 2 节直接从 "The children were nestled…" 跳到 "And mama in her 'kerchief…"，漏收名诗行 *While visions of sugar-plums danced in their heads;*（节目朗读中有），依"以 PDF 为准"原则不补入正文；③ "And filled all the the stockings" 中 the 重复，删去一个；④ 结尾 PDF 作残缺的 `" Christmas to all…`（缺 Happy、缺右引号），据讲解稿还原为 *"Happy Christmas to all, and to all a good-night."*；驯鹿口令节前引号 PDF 未闭合，据原诗补右引号；⑤ PDF 的 *threw up the sash*、*from his head to his foot*、*a right jolly old elf*、*the wild hurricane* 与讲解稿朗读（threw open / toe / right old jolly / hurricanes）有出入，文字以 PDF 为准。

## 三、核心生词与短语（Key Vocabulary）
### 1. stir /stɜː(r)/　v.
- **意思**：（PDF：to move, movement）动弹、活动（诗中用进行时）；日常也指"搅拌"。**搭配**：stir in one's sleep；not stir a finger 一点忙都不帮。**例句**：*Not a creature was stirring.*（连一只小动物都没有动弹。）　**仿写**：*The library was silent; nobody stirred from their seat.*
### 2. hung /hʌŋ/　v.
- **意思**：悬挂（hang 的过去式/过去分词，PDF：把某物固定、仅从上方吊住）。**易错点**：表"悬挂"是 hang-hung-hung；表"绞死"是 hang-hanged-hanged。**例句**：*The stockings were hung by the chimney with care.*（长筒袜小心地挂在烟囱旁。）　**仿写**：*She hung her wet coat behind the door.*
### 3. chimney /ˈtʃɪmni/　n.
- **意思**：烟囱（PDF：让烟排出的垂直通道）。**搭配**：by the chimney 在烟囱旁；sweep a chimney 清扫烟囱。**例句**：*Santa Claus is said to come down the chimney.*（据说圣诞老人会顺着烟囱下来。）　**仿写**：*Smoke rose from the chimney of the small wooden house.*
### 4. nestle /ˈnesl/　v.
- **意思**：依偎；舒适地安顿（PDF：to lie close and snug）。**搭配**：be nestled (together) 依偎在一起；nestle up to sb 依偎到某人身旁。**例句**：*The children were nestled all snug in their beds.*（孩子们暖暖地依偎在床上。）　**仿写**：*The cat nestled in the warm blanket and fell asleep.*
### 5. snug /snʌɡ/　adj.
- **意思**：温暖舒适的、 cozy 的（PDF：warmly comfortable）。**搭配**：snug and warm 暖和舒服；a snug little room 温馨的小房间。**例句**：*The children were nestled all snug in their beds.*　**仿写**：*The little cabin felt snug and warm on the cold winter night.*
### 6. nap /næp/　n.（PDF 标 v.，PDF 勘误）
- **意思**：小睡、打盹（PDF：to sleep for a short time）。诗中 *a long winter's nap* 明确作**名词**，故补 n.。**搭配**：take/have a nap 小睡一会儿。**例句**：*They had settled down for a long winter's nap.*（他们安顿下来睡一个长长的冬觉。）　**仿写**：*My grandfather usually takes a short nap after lunch.*
### 7. clatter /ˈklætə(r)/　n.
- **意思**：咔嗒声、哐当声（PDF：noise，硬物碰撞的嘈杂声）；也可作动词。**搭配**：the clatter of dishes/hooves 盘碟/蹄声的嗒嗒声。**例句**：*Out on the lawn there arose such a clatter.*（草坪上传来一阵哐当声。）　**仿写**：*The clatter of plates came from the busy kitchen.*
### 8. tore /tɔː(r)/　v.
- **意思**：tear（撕、扯）的过去式（PDF：rip），诗中 *tore open* = 猛地拉开。**搭配**：tear open a letter 一把撕开信；tear up 撕碎。**例句**：*Tore open the shutters and threw up the sash.*（猛地拉开百叶窗，推上窗框。）　**仿写**：*He tore open the envelope and read the note quickly.*
### 9. lustre /ˈlʌstə(r)/　n.
- **意思**：光泽、光彩（PDF：radiance；英式拼写，美式作 luster）。**搭配**：the lustre of silk/silver 丝绸/银子的光泽；add lustre to 给……增光。**例句**：*The moon gave the lustre of mid-day to objects below.*（月光给万物镀上正午般的光泽。）　**仿写**：*Her long black hair had a healthy lustre.*
### 10. sleigh /sleɪ/　n.
- **意思**：雪橇（驯鹿或马拉、雪地滑行的大型交通工具）。**PDF 勘误**：PDF 释义只剩 "large"，被截断。**辨析**：sleigh 大、有座；sled 小、多供玩耍。**例句**：*What should appear but a miniature sleigh and eight tiny reindeer.*（出现的竟是一架小雪橇和八只小驯鹿。）　**仿写**：*Santa rides through the sky in a sleigh pulled by reindeer.*
### 11. reindeer /ˈreɪndɪə(r)/　n.
- **意思**：驯鹿（PDF：large deer，释义被截断）。**易错点**：单复数同形，*eight reindeer* 不加 s。**例句**：*Eight tiny reindeer pulled the little sleigh.*（八只小驯鹿拉着小雪橇。）　**仿写**：*Reindeer live in the cold northern regions of the world.*
### 12. rapid /ˈræpɪd/　adj.
- **意思**：迅速的、飞快的（PDF：with high speed，比 fast 更正式）。**搭配**：rapid growth 快速增长；a rapid recovery 迅速康复。**例句**：*More rapid than eagles his coursers they came.*（他的鹿队比雄鹰还快地飞驰而来。）　**仿写**：*The new medicine helped the patient make a rapid recovery.*
### 13. dash /dæʃ/　v.
- **意思**：猛冲、飞奔（PDF：to move with violence; rush）。**搭配**：dash away 飞速离开；dash out 冲出去；dash to the station 冲向车站。**例句**：*Now dash away! dash away! dash away all!*（快跑！快跑！大家一起快跑！）　**仿写**：*He dashed to the station and just caught the last train.*
### 14. hurricane /ˈhʌrɪkən/（美常读 /ˈhɜːrəkeɪn/）　n.
- **意思**：飓风（PDF：very big rain and wind storm，大西洋热带风暴）。**搭配**：a strong/violent hurricane 强飓风；hurricane-force winds 飓风级大风。**例句**：*As dry leaves that before the wild hurricane fly.*（就像在狂虐的飓风前飞舞的枯叶。）　**仿写**：*The hurricane blew down several old trees in the town.*
### 15. obstacle /ˈɒbstəkl/（美 /ˈɑːbstəkl/）　n.
- **意思**：障碍（物）、阻碍（PDF：something that obstructs or hinders progress）。**搭配**：an obstacle to sth ……的障碍；overcome/meet an obstacle 克服/遇到障碍。**例句**：*When they meet with an obstacle, mount to the sky.*（它们一遇到阻挡，便扶摇直上。）　**仿写**：*Fear of making mistakes can be an obstacle to learning English.*
### 16. courser /ˈkɔːsə(r)/　n.
- **意思**：（诗歌古语）骏马、奔马（PDF：a thing suitable for running or chasing）；诗中借指拉雪橇的驯鹿，日常英语已不用此义。**例句**：*More rapid than eagles his coursers they came.*（主持人还原正常语序：*His coursers came more rapidly than eagles.*）　**仿写**：*In old poems, a fast and strong horse is often called a courser.*
### 17. prance /prɑːns/（美 /præns/）　v.
- **意思**：（马等动物）腾跃、欢蹦乱跳（PDF：to dance or move in a lively, spirited manner）。**例句**：*I heard the prancing and pawing of each little hoof.*（我听见一只只小蹄子腾跃、刨挠的声音。）　**仿写**：*The young horse pranced happily around the field.*
### 18. paw /pɔː/　v.（也作 n.）
- **意思**：（动物）用蹄/爪刨、挠（PDF：to strike or scrape with the paws or feet）；作名词指动物的爪子。**搭配**：paw at the door/ground 用爪子挠门/刨地。**例句**：*I heard the prancing and pawing of each little hoof.*　**仿写**：*The dog pawed at the door because it wanted to come in.*
### 19. hoof /huːf/　n.（pl. hooves /huːvz/）
- **意思**：（马、鹿等的）蹄。**PDF 勘误**：PDF 释义作 "an encasing protecting the foot…"，应为 *a casing protecting the foot…*。**例句**：*The prancing and pawing of each little hoof was heard on the roof.*（屋顶上传来小蹄子腾跃刨挠的声音。）　**仿写**：*The horse's hooves made a loud noise on the stone road.*
### 20. St. Nicholas /snt ˈnɪkləs/　n.
- **意思**：圣尼古拉，圣诞老人的正式称呼，昵称 St. Nick。**PDF 勘误**：PDF 释义写作 "Santa clause"，正确拼写是 **Santa Claus**（clause 是"从句、条款"）。**例句**：*I knew in a moment it must be St. Nick.*（我一眼就知道那一定是圣尼克。）　**仿写**：*Children in many countries hang up stockings for St. Nicholas.*
### 21. tarnish /ˈtɑːnɪʃ/（美 /ˈtɑːrnɪʃ/）　v.
- **意思**：（银器等）失去光泽、变晦暗；诗中作"弄脏"讲（PDF：to ruin, make dirty or destroy）。**搭配**：tarnished with ashes and soot 沾满灰烬煤灰；tarnish one's reputation 败坏某人名声。**例句**：*His clothes were all tarnished with ashes and soot.*（他的衣服沾满了灰烬和煤灰。）　**仿写**：*The old silver spoon was badly tarnished after years of use.*
### 22. soot /sʊt/　n.
- **意思**：煤烟、烟炱（烟囱里的黑色粉末，不可数）。**搭配**：as black as soot 像煤灰一样黑；ashes and soot 灰烬与煤灰。**例句**：*His clothes were all tarnished with ashes and soot.*　**仿写**：*The sweeper brushed the black soot out of the chimney.*
### 23. bundle /ˈbʌndl/　n.
- **意思**：捆、包、包袱（PDF：objects gathered or bound together）。**搭配**：a bundle of toys/clothes 一包玩具/衣服；a bundle of firewood 一捆柴火。**例句**：*A bundle of toys he had flung on his back.*（他把一大包玩具甩到背上。）　**仿写**：*The old woman carried a heavy bundle of newspapers on her back.*
### 24. dimple /ˈdɪmpl/　n.
- **意思**：酒窝、笑靥（PDF：微笑时脸颊上出现的小窝）。**搭配**：have dimples (in one's cheeks) 脸颊有酒窝。**例句**：*His eyes – how they twinkled! his dimples how merry!*（他两眼发亮！酒窝多么欢快！）　**仿写**：*The little girl has two sweet dimples when she smiles.*
### 25. stump /stʌmp/　n.
- **意思**：残留的短截；树桩（PDF：树的主干倒下或被砍后留下的下段）；*the stump of a pipe* 指咬在嘴里的烟斗残柄。**搭配**：a tree stump 树桩；the stump of a pencil/candle 铅笔头/蜡烛头。**例句**：*The stump of a pipe he held tight in his teeth.*（他把短烟斗紧紧咬在牙间。）　**仿写**：*The tired hikers sat down on a tree stump to rest.*
### 26. pipe /paɪp/　n.
- **意思**：烟斗（PDF：smoking utensil）；也指管子。**搭配**：smoke a pipe 抽烟斗；light one's pipe 点烟斗；a water pipe 水管。**例句**：*The stump of a pipe he held tight in his teeth.*　**仿写**：*The old man sat by the fire and lit his pipe.*
### 27. wreath /riːθ/　n.
- **意思**：花环、花圈（PDF：用花、枝叶编成的圆环作装饰；复数 wreaths /riːðz/）。**搭配**：a Christmas wreath 圣诞花环；lay a wreath 献花圈。**例句**：*The smoke encircled his head like a wreath.*（烟雾像花环一样绕着他的头。）　**仿写**：*They hung a green Christmas wreath on the front door.*
### 28. plump /plʌmp/　adj.
- **意思**：圆胖的、胖乎乎的、丰满的（PDF：well filled out or rounded；语气客气可爱，无贬义）。**搭配**：chubby and plump 圆滚滚、胖乎乎；plump cheeks 圆鼓鼓的脸蛋。**例句**：*He was chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf.*（他圆滚滚、胖乎乎，真是个快活的老精灵。）　**仿写**：*The baby has a pair of plump little legs.*
### 29. elf /elf/　n.（pl. elves /elvz/）
- **意思**：小精灵（PDF：a magical being；传说中住在北极、帮圣诞老人做玩具的小矮人）。**易错点**：复数是 elves，不是 elfs。**例句**：*He was chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf.*　**仿写**：*In Christmas stories, elves help Santa Claus make toys all year.*
### 30. thistle /ˈθɪsl/　n.
- **意思**：蓟（PDF：a prickly plant，叶带刺，种子带白色冠毛 down，风一吹就散）。**例句**：*And away they all flew like the down of a thistle.*（他们像蓟花的绒毛一样飞走了。）　**仿写**：*The thistle, with its purple flower, is a famous symbol of Scotland.*
### 31. 'twas /twɒz/（美 /twɑːz/）　古语缩写（补充）
- **意思**：= *it was* 的诗歌/古语缩写，现代写作和口语都不用，只见于诗歌、历史故事或戏谑模仿。**例句**：*'Twas the night before Christmas.*（那是圣诞前夜。）　**仿写**：*'Twas a cold and starry night when the travelers finally reached the inn.*（仿写时带文学色彩即可，日常说话请用 It was…。）
### 32. in spite of oneself 　介词短语（补充）
- **意思**：不由自主地、情不自禁地（做了自己本不想做的事）。主持人强调："we say this in English all the time."**搭配**：反身代词随主语变化：in spite of myself / yourself / himself。**例句**：*I laughed when I saw him, in spite of myself.*（我本想绷住，可见到他还是笑了。）　**仿写**：*In spite of myself, I cried at the end of the sad film.*
### 33. peddler /ˈpedlə(r)/　n.（补充）
- **意思**：流动小贩（没有店铺、走街串巷卖货的非正式商贩；英式也拼 pedlar）。节目举例：fruit peddler 卖水果的、watch peddler 兜售手表的。**例句**：*He looked like a peddler just opening his pack.*（他活像个刚打开货包的小贩。）　**仿写**：*A fruit peddler was selling fresh oranges at the street corner.*
### 34. ere /eə(r)/　prep./conj.（补充，古语）
- **意思**：在……之前（= before）；主持人明确说现在口语不用，只见于诗歌和故事。**例句**：*I heard him exclaim, ere he drove out of sight.*（在他消失于视野之前，我听见他喊道。）　**仿写**：*The word ere means "before", and you will meet it mainly when reading old poems.*

## 四、重点句型（Sentence Patterns）

1. **'Twas the night before Christmas, when all through the house…**
   "那是圣诞前夜，整栋房子里……"——'Twas 是 it was 的诗歌缩写，后接时间名词 + when 从句交代故事背景；日常写作请写 *It was…*。
   仿写：*It was the night before the Spring Festival, and the whole family was making dumplings.*

2. **Not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse.**
   "没有一个生灵在动弹，连只老鼠都没有。"——*Not a + 单数可数名词* 构成全部否定；*not even…* 追加一个极端例子加强"万籁俱寂"的语气。
   仿写：*Not a student was late that morning, not even the boys who lived far away.*

3. **More rapid than eagles his coursers they came.**
   "他的鹿队比雄鹰还快地飞驰而来。"——诗歌倒装：比较表语前置，主语后再跟一个同位代词 they；正常语序是 *His coursers came more rapidly than eagles.*（主持人现场还原的句子）。
   仿写：*Quicker than a cat, the little boy climbed up the tall tree.*

4. **As dry leaves that before the wild hurricane fly, …, so up to the house-top the coursers they flew.**
   "正如枯叶在狂风中飞舞……，鹿队也这样飞上了屋顶。"——*As…, so…* 是正式的明喻句式："正如……，……也一样"；as 从句里还嵌了 that 引导的定语从句。
   仿写：*As a river always flows to the sea, so time moves on and never returns.*

5. **He was chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf.**
   "他圆滚滚、胖乎乎的，真是个快活的老精灵。"——逗号后的名词短语 *a right jolly old elf* 作主语补足语（同位语），对主语 he 追加评价；这里的 right 是副词，意为"真正地、十足地"（古语用法）。
   仿写：*My little cousin ran into the room, a bright and cheerful girl.*

6. **I laughed when I saw him, in spite of myself.**
   "我一见他，就不由自主地笑了。"——*in spite of oneself* 是高频口语短语，表示"本不想这样却没忍住"，反身代词随主语变化，常放句末。
   仿写：*I agreed with her in spite of myself, although I was still a little angry.*

## 六、讲解拓展（来自节目讲解）

- **诗歌地位**：这首诗原名 *A Visit from St. Nicholas*（1823 年发表，相传作者为 Clement Clarke Moore），因首行常被称作 *The Night Before Christmas*。主持人介绍它是圣诞节"read or listened to on the holiday"的传统节目，首行 *'Twas the night before Christmas* 和结尾 *Happy Christmas to all* 几乎人人会背；今天大家熟悉的圣诞老人形象（雪地雪橇、钻烟囱、塞长筒袜、浑圆快活）很大程度上由这首诗定型。
- **圣诞老人的一串名字**：St. Nicholas 是正式称呼（源自历史上的圣徒尼古拉），St. Nick 是昵称，Santa Claus 是今天最常用的叫法——三个名字指同一个人。他住在北极（North Pole），因为那里太冷，所以诗中 *dressed all in fur*（一身毛皮）。
- **八只驯鹿与第九只**：诗中驯鹿依次为 Dasher、Dancer、Prancer、Vixen、Comet、Cupid、Donder、Blitzen，每只都有名字，跑得比雄鹰还快。主持人特别提醒：现在常说"九只"，多出来的那只红鼻子 *Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer* 是 20 世纪流行歌曲故事后加的，专门在队首照亮夜空，所以这首更早的诗里找不到他。
- **古语与诗歌语序**：'twas = it was、ere = before（主持人开玩笑说不会再讲 *Ere I was walking to school…*）、kerchief 是 handkerchief 的缩写（包头巾；诗中 mama 戴头巾、爸爸戴 nightcap 睡帽，是两百年前的穿着）。*More rapid than eagles his coursers they came* 是诗中倒装，正常说 *His coursers came more rapidly than eagles*，听讲解时抓住"语序为押韵服务"就不会乱。
- **主持人讲词**：① tarnish 日常多指银器"氧化变暗"，诗中引申为衣服被 ashes and soot（灰烬煤灰）弄脏；② peddler 是没店铺、走街串巷的流动小贩（fruit peddler、watch peddler 兜售假 Rolex 的那种）；③ droll = funny，英式英语还常用，美式口语基本只说 funny；④ chubby and plump 就是"a little bit fat"，但带可爱、讨喜的色彩；⑤ in spite of myself 意为"本想严肃，结果没忍住"，是生活高频短语。
- **Merry 还是 Happy**：节目开头两位主持人互道 *Merry Christmas*（美式主流说法），而诗的结尾是 *Happy Christmas to all*（英式英语至今常用，英王圣诞致辞就说 Happy Christmas）。两种祝福都正确，给英美朋友分别使用会更地道。

## 八、学习建议（CET-4 水平）

1. **大声诵读，感受押韵**：本诗是典型的 AABB 四行押韵体（house/mouse、care/there、clatter/matter…），非常适合练英语节奏。先听节目朗读跟读两遍，再自己按"每行四拍"的节奏朗诵，重点体会 *stirring/mouse*、*clatter/flash* 处的连读和重音，比默读更能记住句子。
2. **古语只"认"不"用"**：'twas、ere、courser、kerchief 这类词能读懂、听出来即可，四级口语和写作里不要使用；把它们和现代说法配对记忆（'twas→it was、ere→before、courser→fast horse），既积累文化词汇又不影响输出准确性。
3. **画一张"圣诞词汇思维导图"**：以 Christmas 为中心，把 chimney、stocking、sleigh、reindeer、soot、wreath、eggnog、snowman、carol、white Christmas 等串成一张图，边指图边念英文并造句，成串记忆节日话题词汇——四级听力和翻译常考"介绍中国节日"，这些词可直接反向迁移到春节介绍。
4. **高频表达口头输出**：*in spite of myself*、*not a …, not even …*、*chubby and plump*、*a bundle of* 各造 1 个与自己生活相关的句子并说出声；再用 5 句话向同伴复述"圣诞老人如何来访又离开"（clatter→sleigh→down the chimney→fill the stockings→up and away），练过去时叙事的完整链条。
