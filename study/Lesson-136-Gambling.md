# Lesson 136 · Gambling（赌博）

> **等级**：Upper-Intermediate（中高级）　**编号**：C0136　**场景**：Global View（市民热议：赌博该不该合法化）
> **学习目标**：学会用英语就争议话题表明立场、分层列举论据、表达强烈反感并在被说服后改变看法，掌握 6 个核心表达：
> `legalize` / `vice industry` / `built on deception` / `exploitation` / `for the sole purpose of` / `be addicted to`

---

## 一、对话原文（Dialogue）

**A = 市民（支持合法化，看重商机）　B = 市民（强烈反对赌博，正筹备抗议集会）**

- **A**: Did you hear? The state is thinking of legalizing gambling in our city! Soon we are gonna have amazing hotels and casinos here which will be good for our business!
- **B**: Are you serious? Gambling is a vice industry built on deception and fed by the intentional exploitation of human weakness for the sole purpose of monetary gain! It disgusts me.
- **A**: What are you talking about? How does it exploit people?
- **B**: Well, to begin with, Gambling is addictive, ruins marriages, destroys families and bankrupts communities. Once you are addicted it is very difficult to stop. People have lost their houses, cars and been left out on the street after becoming addicted. Secondly, it exploits because men become addicted to gambling most often because of the action and risk. Women gamble to escape, and senior citizens will start gambling for the social interaction. Underage gamblers often start gambling on sports with friends and then illegal bookies.
- **A**: Geez! Now that I think about it, maybe legalizing gambling isn't such a good idea! Although, I have been to Las Vegas, and I didn't become addicted or anything like that.
- **B**: You cannot predict who will become addicted to gambling. Now excuse me, I have a protest rally to organize!

## 三、核心生词与短语（Key Vocabulary）

### 1. legalize /ˈliːɡəlaɪz/ 　动词
- **意思**：使合法化（*legal* 合法的 + 后缀 *-ize* 使……化）；反义 *make illegal / ban*；名词 *legalization*。**搭配**：*legalize sth*；介词后用动名词 *think of legalizing gambling*；*the legalization of gambling*；*illegal* 非法的（il- 否定前缀）。**例句**：*The state is thinking of legalizing gambling in our city.*（对话原句）/ *I believe we should legalize drinking at the age of 18.*（节目例句）
- **勘误**：PDF 词性栏写作 "principle verb, present progressive"，应为 **principal verb（实义动词），对话中用现在分词形式**；*principle*（原则）与 *principal*（主要的）是常见易混词。

### 2. vice /vaɪs/ 　名词
- **意思**：恶习、不道德的行为（赌博、酗酒、吸毒等）；*vice industry* 即靠这类恶习赚钱的"罪恶产业"。**用法**：统称恶习时不可数，指具体一项恶习时可数（*Drinking is one of his vices.*）；*vice squad*（警方"风化组"，专扫黄赌毒）。**例句**：*Gambling is a vice industry built on deception.*（对话原句）/ *Addictions like drinking or drug abuse are a type of vice.*（节目例句改写）

### 3. exploitation /ˌeksplɔɪˈteɪʃn/ 　名词（不可数）
- **意思**：剥削、（不正当地）利用；动词 *exploit*。**用法**：*the exploitation of sb/sth*（*the exploitation of human weakness*）；动词有两面：贬义 *exploit employees*（剥削员工）、中性 *exploit natural resources*（开发资源）。**例句**：*Gambling is fed by the intentional exploitation of human weakness.*（对话原句改写）/ *We often hear about companies exploiting their employees in the news.*（节目例句）

### 4. sole purpose /səʊl ˈpɜːpəs/ 　短语
- **意思**：唯一目的（*sole* = only）。**用法**：*the sole purpose of sth is…*；*for the sole purpose of doing sth*。**例句**：*…for the sole purpose of monetary gain.*（对话原句）/ *Nonprofit organizations' sole purpose is to help people.*（节目例句）
- **辨音**：*sole* 与 *soul*（灵魂）同音 /səʊl/，拼法不同。

### 5. monetary /ˈmʌnɪtri/（美 /ˈmɑːnəteri/）　形容词
- **意思**：金钱的、货币的（= related to money，比 money 正式）。**搭配**：*monetary gain*（金钱收益）、*monetary policy*（货币政策）、*monetary value*（货币价值）。**记忆**：**IMF** = *International Monetary Fund*（国际货币基金组织）。**例句**：*The sole purpose of the gambling industry is monetary gain.*（对话原句改写）

### 6. gambling /ˈɡæmblɪŋ/ 　名词（不可数）
- **意思**：赌博（在游戏或比赛中押钱赌输赢）；动词 *gamble*；*gambler* 赌徒。**搭配**：*gamble on sports*（赌体育比赛）、*online gambling*（网络赌博）、*take a gamble (on sth)*（赌一把、冒险）。**例句**：*Men become addicted to gambling most often because of the action and risk.*（对话原句）

### 7. casino /kəˈsiːnəʊ/ 　名词（可数）
- **意思**：赌场（内设轮盘、纸牌等可下注游戏的场所），复数 *casinos*。**勘误**：PDF 词性标 "common noun, plural"，但词头是单数，应为**可数名词单数，复数 casinos**。**例句**：*Soon we are gonna have amazing hotels and casinos here.*（对话原句）

### 8. bookie /ˈbʊki/ 　名词（可数，非正式）
- **意思**：赌注登记人、庄家（*bookmaker* 的口语说法），帮人下注、收钱赔钱，对话中指非法庄家。**勘误**：PDF 词性标 "plural"，但词头是单数，应为**可数名词单数，复数 bookies**。**搭配**：*place/put a bet with a bookie*（通过庄家下注）、*an illegal bookie*、*sports bookie*。**例句**：*Underage gamblers often start gambling on sports with friends and then with illegal bookies.*（对话原句改写）/ *You would go to a bookie to place your bet because there's no legal place to do it.*（节目例句）

### 9. addicted /əˈdɪktɪd/ 　形容词
- **意思**：上瘾的、入迷的（无法停止做有害之事）；名词 *addiction*。**用法**：*be / become / get addicted to sth (or doing sth)*，**to 是介词**，后接名词或动名词：*become addicted to gambling*。**辨析**：*addicted* 说"人上了瘾"；*addictive* 说事物"让人上瘾的"。**例句**：*Once you are addicted, it is very difficult to stop.*（对话原句）

### 10. addictive /əˈdɪktɪv/ 　形容词（补充）
- **意思**：令人上瘾的、容易成瘾的（描述事物本身）。**搭配**：*a highly addictive drug / game*（极易上瘾的毒品/游戏）。**例句**：*Gambling is addictive, ruins marriages, destroys families and bankrupts communities.*（对话原句）

### 11. deception /dɪˈsepʃn/ 　名词（补充）
- **意思**：欺骗、骗局（让别人相信假象）；动词 *deceive*；形容词 *deceptive*（有欺骗性的）。**搭配**：*be built on deception*（建立在欺骗之上）、*obtain sth by deception*（靠骗术获得）。**对话中作用**：B 用 *built on deception* 给赌博业定性。**例句**：*Gambling is an industry built on deception.*（节目原句）

### 12. bankrupt /ˈbæŋkrʌpt/ 　动词 / 形容词（补充）
- **意思**：对话中作**动词**"使破产、使倾家荡产"（*bankrupts communities* 为第三人称单数）；作形容词表示"破产的"。**搭配**：*go bankrupt*（破产）；名词 *bankruptcy*。**例句**：*Gambling destroys families and bankrupts communities.*（对话原句改写）/ *The company went bankrupt last year.*

## 四、重点句型（Sentence Patterns）

1. **Did you hear? The state is thinking of legalizing gambling!**
   "听说了吗？州里正考虑把赌博合法化！"——*Did you hear?* 是分享新闻的地道开场；*think of doing sth* 中 of 是介词，后接动名词。仿写：*Did you hear? They're thinking of opening a new subway line here.*

2. **Are you serious? It disgusts me.**
   "你认真的吗？真让我恶心。"——*Are you serious?* 表示难以置信；*sth disgusts sb* 语气远比 *I don't like it* 强烈；也可说 *be disgusted by/at sth*。仿写：*Are you serious? The idea of cheating on the exam disgusts me.*

3. **Sth is a vice industry built on deception and fed by the intentional exploitation of…**
   "某产业是建立在欺骗之上、靠蓄意剥削……滋养的罪恶产业。"——*built on…*、*fed by…* 两个过去分词短语作后置定语，用比喻给对方定性，议论文高分句式。仿写：*Fake ads are a business built on deception and fed by people's greed.*

4. **To begin with, … Secondly, …**
   "首先……其次……"——分层列举论据的路标词，让长篇发言条理清楚；后文还可接 *Finally / What's more*。仿写：*To begin with, smoking harms your health. Secondly, it wastes a lot of money.*

5. **Once you are addicted, it is very difficult to stop.**
   "一旦上了瘾，就很难戒掉。"——*once*（一旦）引导条件/时间状语从句，从句用一般现在时表将来；*it is difficult to do* 中 it 为形式主语。仿写：*Once you start lying, it is very difficult to stop.*

6. **Now that I think about it, maybe … isn't such a good idea.**
   "现在我再一想，也许……并不是什么好主意。"——*now that*（既然、这一下）引导原因状语；*not such a + 名词* 用来委婉地改变看法。仿写：*Now that I think about it, maybe quitting my job isn't such a good idea.*

## 六、讲解拓展（来自节目讲解）

- **legalize 串起一个文化话题**：*legal* 加 *-ize* 即"使合法化"，加 *il-* 即 *illegal*。主持人借机聊到美国全各州法定饮酒年龄都是 **21 岁**（上世纪 70 年代末统一），而有主持人主张 18 岁就该 *legalize drinking*，理由是"越禁止越不负责任地喝"——写"某事物该不该合法化"类作文可直接借用。
- **exploit 的褒贬两面**：贬义"剥削"（*companies exploit their employees*）与中性"开发、利用"（*exploit the earth for its oil, diamonds and gold*）。本课取贬义：*intentional exploitation of human weakness*（蓄意利用人性弱点）。
- **vice 的文化串联**：基督教 *seven deadly sins*（七宗罪）都是 vices，酗酒、暴食、抽烟、吸毒、赌博都算；美剧 *Miami Vice*（《迈阿密风云》）里的 *vice squad* 是警方"风化组"，专管黄赌毒。
- **sole 与 soul 同音陷阱**：两词都读 /səʊl/，*sole purpose* 的 sole 拼作 S-O-L-E（唯一的），soul（灵魂）拼作 S-O-U-L。*monetary* 看着眼生，记住 IMF = *International Monetary Fund* 立刻就会。
- **bookie 与下注行话**：bookie 是 *bookmaker* 的口语简称，找他 *place a bet / put money down*（下注），通常不合法；合法赌博会去 *casino* 或赛马场。世界三大赌城：Las Vegas（拉斯维加斯）、Monaco（摩纳哥蒙特卡洛）、Macau（澳门）；节目还提到 roulette 押中数字赔率高达 35 倍。
- **可迁移的辩论结构**：抛消息（*Did you hear? … good for business*）→ 扣帽子定性（*vice industry, built on / fed by*）→ 分层论证（*To begin with / Secondly*）并细分人群（men / women / senior citizens / underage gamblers）→ 对方动摇（*Now that I think about it…*）但留小尾巴（*…or anything like that* 我可没有……之类的）→ 金句收口（*You cannot predict who will become addicted*）。主持人个人态度：只要 *in moderation*（适度），政府不该管个人怎么花钱。

## 八、学习建议（CET-4 水平）

1. **长句切分跟读**：B 的发言是本课难点，先按意群切停（*built on deception / and fed by / the intentional exploitation / of human weakness*），逐段跟读再连读，重点模仿 *to begin with … secondly* 的列举节奏和 *It disgusts me* 的强烈语气。
2. **背论据链而不是背单词**：把 *addictive → ruins marriages → destroys families → bankrupts communities → left out on the street* 这条因果链整串背下来，写到任何"恶习的危害"类作文（网瘾、抽烟、酗酒）都能替换套用。
3. **角色扮演 + 换边**：两人分别演 A、B 完整演一遍；然后交换立场，让支持方用 *vice industry / exploitation / to begin with* 反驳，强迫自己反向输出，而不只是念台词。
4. **复习节奏**：第 1 天精听跟读，第 3 天看着中文译文复述英文话轮，第 7 天用 *Did you hear? / to begin with / secondly / now that I think about it* 写一段 80 词小议论文（话题：*Should online gambling be banned?*），把听力输入转成写作和口语的现成弹药。
